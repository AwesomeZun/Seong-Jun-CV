Research profileI am an immunologist working with primary mammalian cells, multiplexed protein and RNA measurements, and quantitative imaging. I would apply donor-aware analysis and held-out evaluation to PRISM assay validation, imaging automation, and drug-response studies in human iPSC-derived neurons.

Position-Relevant Expertise

  • Mammalian cell and tissue workflows. Primary human immune-cell assays, tissue dissociation, cell and nucleus isolation, and automated library preparation on the 10x Chromium Connect.
