Research profileMy research combines experimental immunology, binding assays, and computational analysis of cellular responses. I ran Biacore SPR and ELISA assays for therapeutic antibody selection and coordinated external preclinical studies. I also develop single-cell and multimodal analysis workflows.

Position-Relevant Expertise

  • Binding measurements. I ran Biacore SPR for association and dissociation kinetics, affinity ranking, receptor blockade, and human–primate cross-reactivity. I used ELISA binding, competition, and epitope assays alongside these measurements.
  • Antibody candidate selection. I compared antibody candidates in a humanized anti-CD40 program and followed lead selection with non-human-primate studies. The program supported a registered antibody patent in 2024.
  • CRO coordination. I designed and placed external studies, specified assay conditions, reviewed raw data, and tracked delivery dates. I used the results to make decisions about advancing candidates.
  • Cellular and computational analysis. I used high-parameter flow cytometry, single-cell and spatial profiling, and targeted proteomics to examine cellular responses. I wrote analysis code and tested results in held-out data.
  • Grant writing and project execution. I participated in proposal writing and project execution for industry projects totaling about $1.3 million. I would consider LSRF and K99/R00 applications after confirming eligibility and project restrictions.

Vision-language
and retrieval models
Finetuning.
